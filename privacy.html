Privacy Policy

Identity & Access

Pseudonymous Participation

MoonChat is designed for maximum privacy. Currently, access is granted via a self-chosen username. No email, password, or real-life identity is required to participate in the ecosystem.

Data Protocol

  • 01
    Usernames & Profiles

    We only store the username you choose to identify your messages and settings. This data is not linked to any personal information.

  • 02
    Future Web3 Integration

    We are preparing to integrate Solana Wallet authentication (e.g., Phantom). Once implemented, you will have the option to link your identity to your public wallet address for enhanced security and features.

  • 03
    Zero Tracking

    We do not use invasive tracking cookies or third-party analytics that profile your behavior. Your interactions stay within the MoonChat protocol.

Service Providers

To provide real-time market data and analytics, the extension may communicate with public APIs (such as DexScreener). These interactions are limited to the technical data required to fetch the requested information.

Updates & Contact

As the MoonChat protocol evolves, we will update this policy to reflect new Web3 features. Your continued use signifies your trust in our privacy-first approach.
